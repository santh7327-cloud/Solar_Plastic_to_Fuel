HOW THE CONCEPT WORKS

01 · Sort and inspect feedstock

The concept starts with sorted, characterised material. Contaminants are separated before any process stage; PVC is excluded from this illustrative feed.

!

Safety and engineering boundary
This is a visual learning prototype only. Real pyrolysis requires a professionally engineered sealed reactor, pressure relief, gas detection, vapour treatment, fire protection and qualified supervision. Do not build or heat a homemade reactor.
